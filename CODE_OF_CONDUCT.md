# Code of Conduct

Mini CI is a learning-focused project. Contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and patient.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing experience levels and viewpoints.
- Give and receive feedback graciously.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, and behaviour rather than people.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing someone else's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who do not follow this policy. Report concerns through the repository maintainers or GitHub moderation tools.
